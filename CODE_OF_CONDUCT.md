# Community code of conduct

Lumiqe should be a place where people can learn, disagree respectfully, and contribute safely. This matters especially in a project about appearance and skin tone.

## Our standards

Be kind and specific in reviews. Assume good intent, explain the problem you see, and offer a path forward. Welcome newcomers and respect different backgrounds, identities, and skill levels. Critique the software and evidence, never a person's body or appearance. Respect privacy and do not share another person's photo or personal details without consent.

Harassment, discrimination, sexualized comments, threats, sustained disruption, and publishing private information are not welcome.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the repository owner's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/DineshDhanoki) or use GitHub's report tools. Include a link and a brief description; do not post private details publicly. Maintainers may remove content, request changes in behavior, temporarily limit participation, or ban repeat or severe offenders. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as practical.

This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.
